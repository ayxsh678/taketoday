The topline looks clean: Asian startup funding bounced. Dig one layer down and it looks more like a migration.

First-quarter deal value across the region hit $22.1 billion, up from $17.3B in Q4. That snaps six straight quarters of declines. It's the first unambiguously positive data point venture investors have had since late 2024.

### Where the money actually went

India took the largest share. Late-stage fintech absorbed roughly $3B alone, with climate and industrial tech adding another $2.5B. Japan posted its strongest quarter since 2022, led by enterprise software and robotics.

China did not participate. Deal count fell for the eleventh consecutive quarter. Several pan-Asia funds are, according to two LPs we spoke with, rewriting their mandates to remove China-specific deployment targets rather than fail to meet them.

### Why it matters

Capital isn't disappearing. It's choosing destinations. A year ago the narrative was "Asia is cooling." The more accurate one today is "Asia is sorting." India and Japan have the regulatory clarity, exit pathways, and founder density to absorb the flow. China's absence from the numbers is no longer a lull; it's a pattern.

### What to watch

Whether SoftBank's Vision Fund 3 — which has been quietly sourcing in India and Southeast Asia — announces a formal strategy shift. If it does, the "rebound" narrative gets locked in and everyone else has to reprice.
