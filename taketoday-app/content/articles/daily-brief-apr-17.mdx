Here's what actually matters this morning.

### 1. Fed held, dot-plot moved

Rates stayed at 5.00–5.25%. The quiet news was the Summary of Projections: the median participant now sees two cuts by year-end, up from one. Two-year yields fell fifteen basis points. Growth rotated.

### 2. Nvidia did $40B in a quarter

Data-center revenue up 62% YoY. Gross margin over 74%. Guidance above the street's high end. Post-market reaction: muted. The quiet is the story — analysts are openly asking when hyperscaler capex bends.

### 3. India is consulting on UPI fees

The proposal reinstates MDR on large enterprise-merchant transactions. Modest scope. Enormous implication for any fintech whose unit economics assume UPI is free forever.

### 4. Apple is betting on on-device AI

Most of iOS's upcoming AI features run on the phone itself. A narrow, opt-in cloud tier handles the rest. Privacy is the feature. Silicon is the moat.

### 5. YC's W26 batch got smaller

About 180 companies, down from 230. Older founders. More technical depth. The top of the funnel is tightening, and seed funds usually follow within two quarters.

### The through-line

If you squint, there's one theme running through all of it: the industry is starting to ration. Capital is sorting. Models are getting selective. Regulators are making themselves felt. The shape of the next year looks less like a party and more like a due-diligence meeting.
